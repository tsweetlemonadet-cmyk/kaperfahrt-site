Entry one. Why this ship exists

The successor I waited twenty years for

Somewhere around 2004, a pirate game ate my childhood summers. It never got a worthy successor, so I stopped waiting and started building one.

Kaperfahrt is a living Caribbean. Board an enemy ship and it sails under your flag from that day on. Blockade a harbor and watch grain prices climb, because the economy truly reacts. Hunt the 23 infamous captains of the Blacklist, each convinced they are the main character.

Charted waters

What you will find at sea

Your Fleet

Every ship you capture becomes yours. Up to four sail into battle at your side, in formation, under one flag.

A Living Economy

Ports produce, trade and starve. Cut a supply line and the markets remember who did it.

The Blacklist

23 infamous captains with their own agendas roam the map. Hunt them your way, and take their ships home.

Weather That Matters

Calm, breeze, fresh wind and storm decide how brave you sail. The sea sets the terms.
